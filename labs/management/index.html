Decisions require more than a revenue chart

Review the largest country revenue changes with commercial and operations owners

Evidence: Reconciled country contributions and order/basket decomposition

Still needed: Traffic, conversion, campaign spend, stock availability and contribution margins

Investigate the driver before allocating budget; revenue alone does not establish profitable demand

Audit returns and same-hour reversals separately before changing fulfillment or retention policy

Evidence: Negative return amounts, gross return rate and separately flagged quick reversals

Still needed: Return reasons, product defects, dispatch dates, customer contacts and cost to serve

Confirm mechanisms and costs; aggregate associations do not identify an intervention effect

Use customer-value scores only to propose a controlled retention pilot

Evidence: Separate historical 90-day repeat-purchase evaluation; past-spend baseline won revenue selection

Still needed: Consent, offer costs, randomized holdout, margins and incremental net contribution

Do not treat predicted repeat purchase or high spend as incremental offer response

Definitions and limits

  • Historical transaction totals are descriptive; no sampling confidence interval or causal uplift is claimed.
  • Zero-sale months inside declared coverage are zero observations; a missing source feed must not be declared complete.
  • Country allocation uses existing full-history finance definitions, not point-in-time model features.
  • Guest sales contribute to revenue but cannot support identified-customer retention actions.
  • No margin, acquisition cost, inventory, traffic or intervention outcome is available; revenue is not profit or lifetime value.
