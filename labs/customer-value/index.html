Who will buy again, and how much?

A reproducible 90-day customer prediction study on public UK retail invoices. Models are selected on a past validation window and frozen before two future-period tests.

1,067,371 source lines
UCI Online Retail II, 2009–2011. No employer or live customer data.
180 days of observed behaviour
Recency, order frequency, product variety, gross purchase value and returns.
90-day future outcomes
Repeat purchase and gross product spend. Not subscription churn, profit or lifetime value.

Untouched future-window results

Selected on January 2011 only: logistic for repeat-purchase probability; past_90d for gross spend. Lower Brier score/MAE is better.

The decision this supports

A team with limited review capacity can inspect the highest predicted-value customers first. The CSV includes a deterministic top-20% flag. Choosing a retention offer requires treatment-effect evidence and actual costs; neither is available here.

What keeps the evaluation honest

  • Features stop strictly before the prediction date. Only mature future labels are used in training and model selection.
  • Training windows: July and October 2010. Validation: January 2011. Tests: April and July 2011. Each outcome window is 90 days and disjoint; every snapshot has at least 180 days of source coverage.
  • Customers may recur across time windows: this evaluates future behaviour of an existing customer base, not generalization to unseen customers.
  • The December 9, 2011 shadow score has no observed future outcome. It excludes the partial final source day and demonstrates batch inference only.
  • Gross purchase spend does not subtract future returns, shipping, fees or marketing costs. No revenue uplift, causal effect or realized savings is claimed.
