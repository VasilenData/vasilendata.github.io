What the implementation checks

  • One unique participant per declared assignment; no silent deduplication or unknown arms.
  • Exposure events, when supplied, require known IDs, matching arms and valid time order. They never filter the intention-to-treat denominator.
  • Declared analysis cutoffs reject later assignments and exposures. The public dataset cannot establish observation-window maturity.
  • A complete JSON/HTML/checksum release publishes together. Failed publication preserves prior releases.
